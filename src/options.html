Tasqs Quick Add

Account

Checking…

The extension holds only a token that can add tasks and read today's counts. It cannot read, edit or delete your tasks. Revoke it any time from your profile's Connected apps card.

Ways to add

Toolbar button — click the Tasqs icon on any page; the page's title and link come along.

Keyboard — AltShiftT opens Quick Add while Chrome has focus. Change the shortcut (you can also make it global with CtrlShift1–9).

Right-click — "Add to Tasqs" on selected text, a link, or the page.

Type naturally — call dentist tomorrow 2pm #errands @Personal !high becomes a task with a date, tag, list and priority.

Privacy

Nothing is read from a page until you invoke Quick Add on it, and then only that page's title, address and selected text, sent to tasqs.com over HTTPS to fill in your task. No browsing history, no analytics. Privacy policy
